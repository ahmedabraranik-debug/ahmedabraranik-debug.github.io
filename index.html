About Me

I am an independent filmmaker and director from Bangladesh, and the Founder, Owner & Director of Film Onair , an independent production house built around the idea of meaningful storytelling. Through cinema and visual narratives, I explore human experiences, emotions, and the complexities of contemporary life. My work is driven by a belief that cinema is not merely a form of entertainment, but a language through which stories, ideas, and human truths can be expressed.
